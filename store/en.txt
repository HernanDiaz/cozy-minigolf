DESCRIPTION (4000)
A mini golf game to enjoy at your own pace: lovingly drawn landscapes, gentle music and holes full of surprises.

Swing with the classic three-tap system: start the swing, lock in the power and nail the accuracy right on the line. Easy to learn and very satisfying to master.

FOUR WORLDS
• Breezy Hills: summer meadows, windmills, streams and bouncy mushrooms.
• Spirit Forest: a forest at sunset with fireflies, hollow logs, magic portals and floating spirits.
• Islands in the Clouds: floating islands, bridges with no railings, cloud trampolines and gusts of wind.
• Snowy Summit: slippery ice, soft snow, ice slides and blizzards.

SPECIAL SHOTS
Collect stars to unlock new shots:
• Curve: the ball bends left or right around obstacles.
• Hop: a perfectly timed bounce over fences and gaps.
• Brake: stop the ball dead in its tracks — a must on the ice.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Practice, at your own pace.
• Duel against Kaze, a rival who gets craftier every hole… and won't think twice about knocking your ball away.
• 2 players on the same phone, taking turns.
• Wi‑Fi duel against a friend on the same network.

Plus: golden seeds hidden in every hole, 3 stars per hole, holes in one with confetti and characters who dance when things go well.

No ads, no in-app purchases and no internet connection needed. Available in 15 languages.
