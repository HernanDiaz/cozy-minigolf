It starts on the Home Screen launching the app and shows the typical flow: main menu, choosing world 1, playing several holes against the computer character Kaze (aiming, the three-tap swing, the camera view button, stroke results), the pause menu, returning to the main menu and changing the interface language. The app has no accounts, no login, no user-generated content and no in-app purchases (the app is free; all content is included).

2. Purpose and audience: Cozy Minigolf (Minigolf Relajante) is a relaxing 3D mini golf game for all ages. It offers a calm, stress-free game with no ads, no timers and no data collection. Players progress through 4 worlds (meadows, magic forest, sky islands, snowy peaks), earning stars to unlock new worlds and special shots (Curve, Hop, Brake).

3. How to use it: no setup or login is needed. Open the app, tap a world and play. To swing, tap SWING three times: start, set the power, and set the accuracy. Modes: solo, against the computer character Kaze (DUEL VS KAZE), two players on the same device, or a duel with another device on the same local Wi-Fi network (WI-FI DUEL → CREATE GAME on one device, then join it from the list or with JOIN BY IP on the other). World 1 is unlocked from the start; worlds 2-4 and the special shots unlock with the stars earned on the holes.

4. External services: none. The app works fully offline and uses no third-party SDKs, analytics, ads, authentication, payment processors or AI services. The optional Wi-Fi duel connects two devices directly over the local network (it requests Local Network permission only for that feature).

5. Regional differences: none. The app works the same in all regions; the interface is available in 15 languages (English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Russian, Turkish, Indonesian, Vietnamese, Hindi, Arabic, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese), selectable from the main menu.

6. Regulated industry or third-party material: not applicable. All graphics, music and sounds are generated by the app's own code; there is no licensed third-party content.
